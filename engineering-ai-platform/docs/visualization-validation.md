# Visualization validation record — Release 0.3

The deployed site is available at https://axiomforge-engineering.pages.dev/. The former 2D illustrative canvas was replaced with a Three.js WebGL scene built from dimensioned components for the Mbarara Smart Residence reference: 12.00 m × 10.50 m envelope, two 3.30 m storeys, 230 mm provisional external walls, modeled room zones, doors, windows, structural columns and 16° roof planes with a +8.430 m ridge reference.

The connected browser successfully loaded the real WebGL renderer and displayed the two-storey model with roof planes, interior partitions, floors, openings, shadows, grid, dimension witnesses and selectable envelope geometry. A wall selection showed `upper / North external wall`, category `Envelope`, level `upper`, dimensions `12.00 × 3.12 × 0.23 m`, and material `Provisional rendered masonry`. Measurement mode was exercised by selecting two visible geometry points and returned `MEASURED 0.000 m` and `MEASURED 8.000 m` for the tested points. The deployed UI exposes OrbitControls orbit/pan/zoom, perspective/orthographic camera switching, floor visibility, section cut, explode, material presets and calculation overlays.

Playwright verification against the public hostname exercised technical BIM mode, camera switching, ground/first floor visibility controls, section slider, exploded view, material preset, solar overlay and the live solar solver. Final asserted state included `camera=ORTHOGRAPHIC`, `section=55%`, `exploded=true`, `material=concrete`, `overlaySolar=true`, `generation=8,789.2`, and `solverStatus=SCREENING_PASS`. The public page loaded with zero console errors after the WebGL capability probe was added. The Playwright runtime used for automated checks has WebGL disabled, so it displayed the explicit constrained-browser notice; the connected browser was used to verify the actual WebGL model and geometry selection.

This is a provisional editable reconstruction from a written brief. It is not an exact reconstruction of unavailable drawings and is not construction-approved, code-approved, or a verified physical simulation. Solar and structural overlays are calculation-driven screening views, not validated physical simulations.
