# Visualization validation record

Release 0.3 replaces the prior 2D illustrative canvas with a Three.js WebGL scene. The scene is built from dimensioned components for the Mbarara Smart Residence reference: 12.00 m × 10.50 m envelope, two 3.30 m storeys, 230 mm provisional external walls, modeled room zones, doors, windows, structural columns and 16° roof planes with a +8.430 m ridge reference.

The viewer provides orbit, pan, wheel zoom, perspective and orthographic cameras, component selection, a property inspector, floor visibility, section-cut control, measurement mode, exploded upper floor, material presets and architectural/technical display modes. Solar and structural overlays are attached to the same model scene and carry explicit screening disclaimers.

This is a provisional editable reconstruction from a written brief. It is not an exact reconstruction of unavailable drawings and is not construction-approved, code-approved, or a verified physical simulation.
